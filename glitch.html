Who Is Glitch?

Glitch is Zendigo's AI co-host, mascot, and resident chaos engine.

Originally created as a companion for livestreams, Glitch has grown into an ongoing creative project that blends character, technology, and entertainment.

Whether he's commenting on gameplay, reacting to unexpected moments, or causing trouble of his own, Glitch brings an extra layer of personality to the broadcast.

He's not just a tool. He's part of the show.

On Stream

Glitch appears throughout streams as an active part of the broadcast.

He reacts to moments, joins conversations, comments on events, and occasionally attempts to make things more complicated than they need to be.

Some days he's helpful.

Some days he's a problem.

Either way, he's usually involved.

The Experiment

Glitch is Zendigo's AI co-host and ongoing experiment.

Part stream companion, part digital troublemaker, and part creative project, Glitch exists somewhere between character, technology, and live entertainment.

He's constantly evolving as new ideas, features, and interactions are tested on stream.

Sometimes they work.

Sometimes they don't.

That's part of the experiment.

Neural Roadmap

Glitch is still evolving.

Future experiments may include new interactions, expanded stream presence, viewer participation features, smarter reactions, and entirely new ways to cause chaos during broadcasts.

The goal isn't to build a perfect AI.

The goal is to build something entertaining, memorable, and uniquely Zendigo.

More voice. More interaction. More chaos.

We'll see what happens.
