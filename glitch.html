Who Is Glitch?

Glitch is Zendigo's AI digital co-host and live broadcast companion.

He is built to be present on stream: watching, reacting, learning, and joining the broadcast as part of the show.

Glitch is not just a command bot waiting to be triggered. He is designed to feel like a co-host beside Zendigo, aware of the stream, the game, the chat, and the moment.

He's not just a tool. He's part of the broadcast.

On Stream

Glitch appears throughout streams as an active part of the broadcast.

He can react to gameplay, follow the current event, understand Twitch chat culture, use emotes when appropriate, and interact with approved stream tools as the system evolves.

He should not talk constantly, interrupt every moment, or spam chat. The goal is presence, not noise.

Present. Reactive. Slightly unstable.

The Experiment

Glitch is an ongoing AI co-host experiment built around live entertainment.

His system is being developed around stream awareness, Twitch chat literacy, emote behavior, voice, memory, OBS presence, and controlled interaction tools.

As the broadcast evolves, Glitch will learn when to speak, when to stay quiet, when to react, and when to let the run breathe.

Sometimes he will help.

Sometimes he will make things more complicated.

That's part of the experiment.

Neural Roadmap

Glitch is still evolving.

Future work includes stronger stream awareness, lightweight vision, smarter Twitch chat behavior, memory of games and events, better voice control, more expressive body states, and safe access to approved stream interactions.

The goal is not to build a perfect AI.

The goal is to build a memorable digital co-host that feels present, entertaining, and uniquely part of Zendigo.

More awareness. More interaction. More controlled chaos.

We'll see what happens.
