Flappy Face

Controller: connect and press a button

Rotate to landscape for a larger playfield.

How To Play

Press Space, click, or tap to flap. Avoid the signal gates. Tacos appear every fifth gate and add five points.

Controls

Keyboard: Space / W / ↑ / Enter = Flap · R = Restart · M = Mute · S = Sound
Touch: Tap the game = Flap
Controller: A / Cross, X / Square, shoulder, trigger, D-pad ↑ or stick ↑ = Flap
B / Circle or View = Restart · Y / Triangle = Mute · Start = Sound
