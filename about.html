What Is Zendigo?

Zendigo is a gaming and entertainment channel focused on retro games, speedruns, community events, game discoveries, and the occasional dose of chaos.

Whether it's chasing a new personal best, testing a strange demo, running a community challenge, or building something new with Glitch, the goal is simple:

Create entertaining experiences, build a welcoming community, and have fun doing it.

The channel continues to grow, evolve, and experiment with new ideas, but at its core, Zendigo is about sharing the journey.
